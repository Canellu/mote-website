# Mote mascot artwork

These five WebP stills are copied unchanged from
`mote-desktop/src/assets/mascot/moth-*.webp` for the 0.12.0 release.

- Source: Mote's own moth mascot, generated for Mote Desktop from the approved
  Dusk moth concept. Prompts and originals are retained in the desktop
  repository's `output/moth-styles-2026-09-22/`.
- Author: Mote Desktop.
- Licence: owned by Mote Desktop for use in the product and its marketing.
- Processing: transparent cutouts, resized to 256px and encoded as WebP by
  the desktop asset packer. These are the app's actual style-picker stills,
  not screenshots of a running widget.
