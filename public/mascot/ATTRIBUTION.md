# Mote mascot artwork

The WebP stills and animation sheets are copied unchanged from
`mote-desktop/src/assets/mascot/moth-*.webp` for the 0.12.0 release. Playback
uses the app's six-column, eight-row atlas and its original frame timings.

- Source: Mote's own moth mascot, generated for Mote Desktop from the approved
  Dusk moth concept. Prompts and originals are retained in the desktop
  repository's `output/moth-styles-2026-09-22/`.
- Author: Mote Desktop.
- Licence: owned by Mote Desktop for use in the product and its marketing.
- Processing: transparent cutouts, with 256px stills and 160px animation
  cells encoded as WebP by the desktop asset packer. These are the app's
  actual artwork assets, not screenshots of a running widget.
